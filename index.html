01 — Sarantari

Small coves west of Hersonissos. A short swim, then move on.

02 — Malia, then walk

The other palace, if you still want stone. From Potamos Beach a walking path runs to Sisi — headland, Agia Varvara, harbour. Not a drive.

The path is the point. Sisi is only where it ends.

03 — Kolokytha

Pale cove. Park at Agios Loukas. A marked loop goes around the peninsula — the old island of Kalydon — 7–10 km, two to three hours.

04 — Spinalonga

Venetian fortress, then Greece’s last leper colony, closed 1957. Shortest boat from Plaka. Elounda harbour is longer and prettier. No shade. Go early.
