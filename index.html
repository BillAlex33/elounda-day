01 — Sarantari

West of Hersonissos the coast breaks into small coves. A swim, a short walk on the rock, then move on.

02 — Malia, then walk

If Knossos and Phaistos were enough stone, skip this. If not: Malia is the other palace that still holds. The sea is immediately behind it.

From Potamos Beach the way to Sisi is a walking path — east along the water, over the headland, past Agia Varvara, into the harbour. Not a road.

The path is the point. Sisi is only where it ends.

03 — Kolokytha

Skip the ruins. Park at Agios Loukas, walk five hundred metres down to a pale cove. Morning, or after three.

A marked loop goes around the Kolokytha peninsula — the old island of Kalydon — from the Elounda windmills and salt pans, past the chapel, the beach, and back over the hills. About 7–10 km, two to three hours. Little shade.
